📖 Seedance 提示词指南

掌握高质量视频提示词的核心规则

📐 提示词结构

[镜头语言] + [主体描述] + [动作/运动] + [场景/环境] + [光线氛围] + [视觉风格] + [质量词]
示例：
Slow push-in medium shot. A young woman in a white linen dress walks slowly through a lavender field at golden hour. Her hair flows gently in the warm breeze. Soft golden backlight creates a dreamy rim glow. Wide rolling hills in background. Cinematic, shallow depth of field, film grain, romantic and serene atmosphere.

💡 核心规则

✅ 使用英文
Seedance 对英文提示词响应更好，用词精准具体
✅ 具体胜于抽象
❌ beautiful scene
✅ golden sunset over misty mountains
✅ 镜头描述放前面
将镜头语言作为提示词的开头，模型响应更稳定
✅ 长度控制 50-150 词
过长会使模型混乱，过短效果不稳定
❌ 避免负面描述
不用 "no X" "without X"，直接描述想要的
❌ 避免矛盾指令
不要同时要求 static shot 和 camera movement

🎨 常用质量词组合

电影级别
cinematic, film grain, 4K, anamorphic, shallow depth of field

自然纪录片
documentary style, natural light, raw, authentic, 4K nature
产品展示
studio lighting, ultra-detailed, 8K, product photography, clean background
